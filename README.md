# Manuscript and data: Structure-motivated phantom-crossing dark energy: joint constraints from DESI DR2, supernovae and growth

Z. Xu (2026), *Structure-motivated phantom-crossing dark energy: joint constraints from DESI DR2, supernovae and growth*, Research Square preprint, [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-11371459/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11371459/v1).

This repository holds the manuscript and the plotted data behind the figures and tables of the paper. The same files are archived on Zenodo.

## Major findings (dark energy and structure growth)

- The dark-energy density follows ρ<sub>DE</sub> = K⟨a<sub>p</sub><sup>2</sup>⟩/G with K ≈ 4, where ⟨a<sub>p</sub><sup>2</sup>⟩<sup>1/2</sup> ≈ 10<sup>−10</sup> m s<sup>−2</sup> is the mass-weighted rms dark-matter particle acceleration, and its background law ρ<sub>DE</sub> ∝ ⟨a<sub>p</sub><sup>2</sup>⟩(D/D<sub>0</sub>)<sup>n</sup> is driven by the linear growth factor D.
- Matching the linear bulk flow to the nonlinear virial flow at their crossover, a<sub>*</sub> ≈ 0.64, gives a halo acceleration a<sub>c0</sub> ≈ 0.16 cH<sub>0</sub>, close to Milgrom's a<sub>0</sub>.
- In its scale-free limit the law gives an equation of state linear in the growth rate f = d ln D/d ln a, w = −1 − (n/3)(f − f<sub>×</sub>), which stays bounded and crosses w = −1 where f = f<sub>×</sub>.
- Against DESI DR2 BAO, the DESI compressed CMB likelihood, Pantheon+ supernovae and SDSS growth rates, the structural law improves on ΛCDM by Δχ² = −8.4 to −9.7 and on CPL by 0.6–1.9.
- Both structure-growth-tied families have higher Bayesian evidence than CPL and better predict supernovae withheld from the fit.
- 97–100% of posterior histories have w<sub>0</sub> > −1 and a single density maximum at z ≈ 0.4–0.8; the exponent n is degenerate with the acceleration clock and the halo-assembly timing.
- The coupling is postulated, and all results are conditional on the compressed CMB likelihood and the acceleration calibration.
